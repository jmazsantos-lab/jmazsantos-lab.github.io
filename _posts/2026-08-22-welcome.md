After nearly a decade working in packaging, brewing and the FMCG industry, I wanted to share with people what my curiosities are within this industry, how I optimize my work, and some personal thoughts on important news that impact my job and my industry.

My objective with this blog is to create a history of how I evolve as a professional and to show other people and myself a way to navigate this career.

In this blog you will find posts related to:

- Continuous Improvement
- Brewing
- Manufacturing Excellence
- AI and how it can be a great tool for FMCG Professionals
- News from the Industry
- Personal Experiences

Thank you for being here
