After nearly a decade working in packaging, brewing and the FMCG industry, I wanted to share what my curiosities are within this industry, how I optimise my work, and some personal thoughts on the news that impacts my job and my industry.

My objective with this blog is to build a history of how I evolve as a professional, and to show other people — and myself — a way to navigate this career.

You'll find posts on:

- Continuous improvement
- Brewing
- Manufacturing excellence
- AI, and how it can be a great tool for FMCG professionals
- News from the industry
- Personal experiences

Thanks for being here.
